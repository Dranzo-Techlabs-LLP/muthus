( About Muthu's )

A legacy of celebration, built one event at a time.

What started in 1998 as a small catering outfit with a single grill has grown into a full-service event company trusted by families and businesses alike. Muthu still walks every venue, tastes every menu and stays until the last guest leaves.

Our promise is simple: personal attention, honest pricing and flawless execution — whether it's an intimate gathering of fifty or a grand affair for five thousand.
